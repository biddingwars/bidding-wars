## Upgrading the US map imagery

`test/build-us-map.js` takes any equirectangular (plain longitude/latitude)
world image. To use NASA Blue Marble instead of the Natural Earth stand-in:

1. Download a Blue Marble mosaic from NASA's Earth Observatory / Visible Earth
   (for example the 21600×10800 *Blue Marble Next Generation with topography*, or
   the A1/B1 tiles that cover North America). Credit: *NASA Earth Observatory*.
2. `npm i us-atlas topojson-client d3-geo sharp` somewhere, then
   `NODE_PATH=<that>/node_modules node test/build-us-map.js <image> .`
3. Bump `CACHE` in `sw.js` and commit the three `.webp` files.

Nothing in the game changes: the outlines are registered to the projection, not
to the picture. (The sandbox this was built in could not reach NASA's servers,
which is why the stand-in is in place.)

## Eagle audio

A recorded eagle/hawk call was not used: the clip offered for this project had no
clear licence, so the game synthesises its own. If you want a real recording,
use a clip with a clear licence (CC0, or NASA/US-government), keep it flat at the
repo root, add it to `SHELL` in `sw.js`, bump `CACHE`, and credit it above.
