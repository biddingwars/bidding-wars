## Regenerating the US map imagery

`test/build-us-map.js` takes any equirectangular (plain longitude/latitude) world image and warps it to the map's projection; the outlines (`us-map.js`) do not change.

1. Download the image: `https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/73000/73751/world.topo.bathy.200407.3x21600x10800.jpg` (about 27 MB; Visible Earth, *Blue Marble: Next Generation w/ Topography and Bathymetry*, July). Check its sha256 against the table above.
2. `npm i us-atlas topojson-client d3-geo sharp` somewhere, then `NODE_PATH=<that>/node_modules node test/build-us-map.js <image> .`
3. Bump `CACHE` in `sw.js` (and `CONFIG.version`) and commit the three `.webp` files (about 97 KB in all).

(The first version of the map used a Natural Earth shaded-relief stand-in; the December Blue Marble was tried and rejected because its snow cover washes out the northern states and the gold highlight.)

## Eagle audio

A recorded eagle/hawk call was not used: the clip offered for this project had no
clear licence, so the game synthesises its own. If you want a real recording,
use a clip with a clear licence (CC0, or NASA/US-government), keep it flat at the
repo root, add it to `SHELL` in `sw.js`, bump `CACHE`, and credit it above.
