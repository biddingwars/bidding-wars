# US States: fun facts and where each one comes from

Each state has one kid-friendly fun fact, shown on the SOLD screen and in the results. A fact never mentions
population or population rank (population stays hidden until the end), and none is about politics.

## Verification status: please read

**Every fact below is UNVERIFIED.** The build environment had no access to the web, so I wrote each fact from
general knowledge and listed the official source where it can be checked. Nothing here was looked up live.
Treat the "Source to check" column as a to-do list for a human reviewer, not as a citation I confirmed.
Facts I am least sure of (worth checking first): **Kansas** (center-of-48-states monument), **New Jersey**
("said to have been played" first organized baseball game), **Minnesota** (lake count), **Missouri** and **Wyoming**
(superlatives), **Hawaii** (Mauna Kea measured from the sea floor), **Idaho** (waterfall heights),
**Mississippi** (Ojibwe meaning), **Maryland** and **Oklahoma** (wording).

Languages: English only. The other four languages show the English fact (the engine falls back automatically).
Translating them is straightforward once the English wording is reviewed.
