Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Bidding Wars is a country-auction game. This policy covers the app and this website in full — there is nothing else to disclose.

What we collect

Nothing. Bidding Wars has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or tracking software of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere, to us or to anyone else.

What's stored on your device

Three things, saved locally on your device only: the language you picked, your record against the bot, and the name you last entered. None of it ever leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes all three. You can also clear them at any time from the in-app privacy screen's "Delete all data" option.

Nicknames

Player nicknames are optional and are only used to label players on screen during a round. The one you enter for yourself is remembered locally as a convenience for next time, per the storage note above; it is never transmitted anywhere.

Advertising and third parties

No advertising, no tracking, and no third-party software development kits are bundled with this app.

Children

Because Bidding Wars collects no personal data from anyone, of any age, there is nothing to collect from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact details on the support page.
