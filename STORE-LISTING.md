## Description (4,000 at most)

    Every country is for sale. The catch: you can't see how many people live there until the auction is over.

    Bidding Wars is an auction game about the world. Random countries come up one at a time. Every million inhabitants is one point, but the population stays blacked out until the very end. Bid with your head, bluff with your nerve, and spend your budget wisely.

    HOW IT WORKS
    • Bidding opens at €1. On your turn you raise or drop out.
    • Drop out, and whoever holds the top bid buys the lot at that price. Raising is a weapon: push a rival up and they are stuck with a terrible deal. Push too far and you are the one stuck.
    • Money is scarce. Spend it all early and you can only watch when China comes up.
    • At the end every bar is lifted, one by one. Points decide the winner, and efficiency (euros per million people) shows who shopped best.

    THREE PACKS
    • Countries: all 196 countries. Beginner draws from Europe, Medium from every country above 15 million people, Expert from all of them.
    • US States: all 50 states, in dollars.
    • Capital Cities: 195 capitals and their cities' populations.

    PLAY YOUR WAY
    • Solo against one to three bots. Each rolls a fresh walk-away limit for every lot, so you can never quite read it, and it is not shy about telling you what it thinks of your bids.
    • Multiplayer on one phone or iPad: up to four seats, people and bots together.
    • 5 to 30 lots per auction, three difficulty levels, a decision clock you can change, and practice helpers if you want them.
    • Your record against the bot, kept for each pack.

    MADE WITH CARE
    • A 3D globe of everything you bought at the end of each game.
    • Haptics, sound and smooth motion; dark and light looks; larger text; reduce motion.
    • Thirteen languages: English, Deutsch, Italiano, Español, Français, Português (Brasil), Bahasa Indonesia, 简体中文, हिन्दी, 日本語, 한국어, العربية, ไทย.
    • Works fully offline. No account, no ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases. Nothing you do leaves your phone.

## Review notes (App Review Information → Notes)

    Bidding Wars is a single-device game. No sign-in, no account, no network: it runs fully offline (please feel free to test in airplane mode).

    To play: tap "Solo", then "Open the auction". Countries come up one at a time; raise or drop out. After the last lot, tap the bars (or "Lift every bar") to see the result.

    The in-game money is play money that exists only inside one auction. Nothing is sold, there are no ads and no in-app purchases.

    Settings → "Record my games" (off by default) keeps a game log on the device only; the player can share it themselves for beta testing. Nothing is sent anywhere.
