Support

Bidding Wars is a country-auction game: random countries come up as lots, you bid against a rival, and population decides the score once every lot is revealed.

Report a bug or ask a question

The fastest way to reach us is to open an issue on GitHub — we read every one.

Common questions

Does the game need an internet connection?
No — everything runs offline, including the bot, the borders, and the fonts.

Do you collect any of my data?
No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I reset my stats?
Open the menu in-game and use the privacy screen's "Delete all data" option.
