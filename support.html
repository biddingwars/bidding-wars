Support

Bidding Wars is an auction game: countries, US states or capital cities come up as lots, you bid against bots or friends on the same device, and population decides the score once every lot is revealed.

Report a bug or ask a question

Open an issue on GitHub and we will answer there. Please say which device you use and the version number shown at the bottom of the game’s menu.

Common questions

Does the game need an internet connection?
No. Everything runs offline, including the bots, the maps and the globe.

Do you collect any of my data?
No. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. See the privacy policy.

How do I reset my record or delete my data?
On the menu, tap the lock button to open the privacy screen and choose “Delete all data”. To reset only your record against the bot, tap the record on the menu and choose “Reset record”.

Why did a game not count for my record?
Only solo games with the default bot style count. If you changed the bot style or switched on “Show the fair price” in Settings, the game is a practice game.

There is no sound.
Check the sound button at the top of the screen, your device’s volume and its silent switch.

Is the money real?
No. The budget is play money that exists only inside a round. Nothing can be bought, won or cashed out.
